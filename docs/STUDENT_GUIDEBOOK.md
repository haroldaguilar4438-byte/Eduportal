# EduPortal — Student & Parent Guidebook

**Datamex College of Saint Adeline**
Web-Based Integrated School Management System

This guide walks you through everything you can do in EduPortal. You do not need
any technical knowledge — just a browser and your login details.

> **Parents:** you will see the same menus as a student, but everything is scoped
> to the children linked to your account. Use the **Viewing on behalf of** switcher
> near the top of a page to move between your children.

## 1. Signing in for the first time

### If you are new

1. Open the portal and choose **Create account**.
2. Fill in your details, then verify your email — either click the link we send
   you (**valid 24 hours**) or type the **6-digit code** we text/email you
   (**valid 10 minutes**).
3. Sign in. Your account stays pending until the school approves it.

### If you already have an account

1. Choose **Sign in** and enter your email and password.
2. If you have turned on two-factor authentication, enter your 6-digit code from
   your authenticator app.

### I forgot my password

Choose **Forgot password** and enter your email. We send a reset link that is
valid for **1 hour**. Click it, set a new password, then sign in normally.

### My account is locked

After **5 wrong attempts**, the account locks for **15 minutes** for safety. Wait
for the timer to pass and try again — the lock clears by itself.

### Signing out

Click your name at the top-right → **Sign out**, or use the **Sign out** button at
the bottom of the sidebar.

> Your session ends automatically after **30 minutes** of inactivity, so always
> sign out on a shared computer.

## 2. Getting around

### On a computer

- **Sidebar (left)** — every module you can access. Items are grouped by
  section: Main, Enrollment, Academics, Records, Account.
- **Top bar** — search, current school year, notifications, and your account menu.

### On a phone

- Tap the **☰ menu button** at the top-left to open navigation.
- Tap any item to go there — the menu closes automatically.
- Tap the **×** button, or tap outside the menu, to close it.

### Finding things quickly

- Use the **search box** in the top bar. Start typing a name, student number or
  reference and pick from the suggestions.
- The **bell icon** shows your unread notifications.

## 3. Enrolling

### Before you start

Have these ready (the form tells you which ones apply to your level):

| Document | Who needs it |
|---|---|
| PSA Birth Certificate | Everyone |
| Certificate of Good Moral | Most levels |
| 2×2 ID Photo | Everyone |
| Form 138 / Report Card | Basic education, transferees |
| Form 137 (Permanent Record) | Transferees, if available |
| ECCD Checklist / Preschool Record | **Kinder and Grade 1 only** |

> College students are **not** asked for an ECCD checklist or report card —
> the system only requests what your level actually requires.

### Steps

1. Open **Enroll** from the sidebar.
2. Choose the school year, the grade level / year level, and your applicant type
   (new, returning, or transferee).
3. Fill in your personal details and upload the requested documents.
4. Press **Submit**.
5. Follow the progress any time under **My Applications**.

## 4. Checking your application status

Open **My Applications**. Each application shows a status badge.

| Status | Meaning | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending** | Waiting for Accounting to check | Nothing — wait |
| **Accounting approved** | Finance cleared it; payment comes next | Nothing yet |
| **Awaiting payment** | Approved but tuition not paid | Pay to continue |
| **Action required** | Needs a correction or a document | Read the note and fix it |
| **Approved** | Fully approved — you are enrolled | Register for your subjects |
| **Rejected** | Declined | Contact the Registrar |

> Once you are approved, subjects, permits and clearance unlock automatically.

## 5. Uploading documents

Open **Enrollment Documents**. This is your permanent document store.

### The rule that saves you time

> Upload a document **once**. EduPortal keeps it and reuses it automatically for
> every future application. You will be asked to upload again **only** when the
> status says `needs_resubmission` (Accounting gives a reason) or when a required
> item is still missing.

### Uploading

1. Open **Enrollment Documents**.
2. In **Upload**, choose the document type.
3. Choose your file — **PDF, JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB**.
4. Press **Upload new version**. You will see it in the status table below.

### Statuses you will see

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Verified** | Accepted — reused automatically, never ask again |
| **Submitted** | Received, waiting for Accounting |
| **Needs resubmission** | Upload a new version; the reason is shown next to it |
| **Rejected** | Refused — ask Accounting why |

Every upload is kept as a **new version**, so your previous files are never
deleted — see **Version history** on the same page.

## 6. Requesting official documents

Open **Documents** (Academic Documents) for stamped papers:

- Transcript of Records
- Certificate of Enrollment
- Certificate of Grades
- Grade Slip
- Grade Equivalency
- Registration Form

### Steps

1. Choose the **document type**, **school year** and **term**.
2. Add an optional purpose (it helps the Registrar process it faster).
3. Press **Submit request**. Your request appears in the list with a reference
   number like `DOC-2026-AEEBA0`.

### Tracking your request

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | The Registrar has not looked at it yet |
| **Approved** | Accepted, being prepared |
| **Ready** | Printed, awaiting release |
| **Released** | You can open and print it |
| **Rejected** | Refused — a reason is shown; you can request again |
| **Cancelled** | You cancelled it |

- You may **cancel** a request while it is still *Pending*.
- If it is *Rejected*, use **Request Again** — the original is kept in history.
- Only **Released** documents can be printed.

> Some documents (Grade Slip, Transcript, Certificate of Grades, Grade
> Equivalency) require **finalized grades** first. If none exist yet, the system
> tells you to wait for your teachers to submit and the Registrar to finalize.

## 7. Paying

### Steps

1. Open **My Applications** and find your approved application.
2. Press the **Pay** option. It shows your exact amount.
3. Choose a method: **GCash · Maya · Card · QR Ph**.
4. Confirm. You will be redirected to the payment page.
5. After paying, the status becomes *Processing* until the school approves it.

### Payment methods and plans

- The amount follows the **active tuition plan** set by the school — pay in full
  or follow the plan, whichever is available for your application.
- After the school approves, you receive your **receipt by email**.
- Your balance is always visible under **Ledger**.

### My payment is not updating

Payments are confirmed by the school before they show as paid. If it has been
more than a few minutes, check the status in **My Applications**, then contact
Accounting with your reference number.

## 8. Joining classes

You can join subjects **only when fully enrolled and paid**.

1. Open **Class Offerings**. Only subjects matching your level, program and
   term are shown under **Eligible Subjects**. Use **Browse All** to view others
   (read-only).
2. Ask your teacher for the **invite code** (for example `X7K9M2`).
3. Enter the code, preview the class, then confirm.
4. Your joined subjects appear under **My Classes**, with your schedule,
   assignments and submissions.

### I can't join a subject

| Reason | Fix |
|---|---|
| Not yet enrolled / paid | Complete enrollment and payment first |
| Code invalid or wrong | Ask your teacher for the correct code |
| Subject is full | Wait for a slot or ask the teacher |
| Not in your level | Use **Browse All** to view it, but only eligible subjects can be joined |

You can **drop** a subject from **My Classes** — the slot is released for
someone else, so check with your teacher first.

## 9. Checking your grades

Open **Grades** for released grades, and **My Classes** for everything in
progress.

### Why a grade may not be showing

A grade becomes official only after:

```
Teacher enters it  →  submits  →  Registrar reviews  →  FINALIZED
```

Before finalization it is a draft, and drafts are never shown as official.
Grades are also **released** separately — so "finalized" and "visible to you"
are two different steps.

**If your Grades page is empty:** your teacher probably hasn't finalized that
period yet, or it hasn't been released. Ask your teacher, then check again.

### Computing your GWA

The system computes your General Weighted Average from finalized, graded
subjects. Incomplete (INC) subjects are excluded until completed.

## 10. Assignments and submissions

1. Open **My Classes** and select a subject.
2. Find the task under **Assignments**.
3. Press **Open classroom**, read the instructions, then upload your file
   (**PDF, JPG, PNG, WebP**, max 10 MB) and press **Submit**.
4. Your teacher grades it and returns feedback.

### Tips

- Upload **before the due date** — past-due tasks may be marked late.
- You can resubmit if your teacher allows it; submissions are versioned.
- Check **Notifications** for feedback — you get a message when work is graded.

## 11. Exam permits

You need a permit for each exam: **Prelim → Midterm → Prefinal → Finals**. They
must be requested in that order.

### Steps

1. Open **Exam Permits** once you are fully enrolled and paid.
2. Choose the exam period and press **Request**.
3. Track the progress — each permit passes through:

```
Waiting for Accounting  →  Waiting for the Registrar  →  Waiting for your Teacher  →  Approved
```

4. Once **Approved**, open the permit to view or print it.

### Requirements

- Your account must have **no outstanding balance** for the period.
- You must be **fully enrolled**.
- The previous period's permit must be approved first.

## 12. Clearance

Clearance has **two parts** and both must finish.

**1. Academic** — your teachers sign off each subject:

```
Waiting on teachers  →  All signed  →  Registrar reviews  →  Issued
```

**2. Financial** — Accounting clears your account:

```
Pending  →  For review  →  Cleared   (or Not cleared / On hold)
```

### Steps

1. Open **Clearances** and press **Request** for the period you need.
2. Your teachers receive the request and sign their subjects.
3. After all signatures, the Registrar reviews it.
4. Accounting then completes the financial side.

> Start early — this depends on other people responding.

## 14. Securing your account

Open **Security** from the sidebar.

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Change password** | Set a strong, unique password |
| **Two-factor authentication** | Add an extra code from an authenticator app |
| **Recovery codes** | 10 single-use codes if you lose your phone |
| **Trusted devices** | See devices that skip verification; revoke any you don't recognise |
| **Active sessions** | See where you're signed in; revoke others remotely |

### Safer habits

- Never share your password, OTP, or recovery codes — **with anyone**, including
  school staff.
- Use a different password than your email account.
- Sign out on shared computers.
- If someone asks for your code, it is not the school. Report it.

## 15. When something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Page shows an error | Refresh once. If it continues, note the **Reference** code and report it |
| Can't see my grades | See [Checking your grades](#9-checking-your-grades) |
| Submitted something but nothing changed | Some steps wait for staff. Check the status first |
| Payment not updating | Give Accounting a few minutes, then your reference number |
| Login says locked | Wait 15 minutes after 5 failed attempts |
| Verification link expired | Request a new link — links last 24 hours, codes 10 minutes |
| Forgot password | Use **Forgot password** (link valid 1 hour) |
| A menu item is missing | You only see what your role allows. Ask the office if you need more access |
| Chat assistant not responding | It falls back to offline help automatically — try again in a moment |

**When reporting a problem, always include:** your user number or student number,
the page you were on, what you clicked, what you expected, and the Reference
code from any error screen.

## 16. Asking the AI assistant

Tap the **💬 button** in the bottom-right corner on any page.

- Type a question and press **Enter** (use **Shift + Enter** for a new line).
- Tap a suggestion chip to ask a common question instantly.
- **⟳** at the top of the chat starts a fresh conversation.

### It knows your real situation

The assistant can see **your own** records — your enrollment status, your
balance, your permit and document statuses, and your subjects — so its answers
reflect what is actually on your account rather than generic advice.

### What it will not do

- It **cannot** submit applications, make payments, change grades, or issue
  documents. It explains and guides; **you** perform every action.
- It **cannot** see other students' or families' records.
- It **never** asks for your password, OTP, recovery codes, or card details.
- If something is not in your account, it tells you where to check instead of
  guessing.

### It remembers

You don't have to repeat yourself — "monthly?", "then what?", or "why?" will be
understood as follow-ups to your previous question.
